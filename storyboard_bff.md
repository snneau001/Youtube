# "Dog & Cat: Best Friends" — Storyboard

Original short-form animated video, ~32 seconds, 24 fps, 1280x720 (16:9).
Companion piece to "Dog & Cat: Funny Fights" — same characters (Rex the
dog, Whiskers the cat), same rigs, but recolored white and in a warm,
wholesome, no-conflict tone: happy chasing, then sharing a meal.

Characters:
- **Rex** — now a fluffy white dog with a red collar (was golden).
- **Whiskers** — now a solid white cat with a little blue bow on one ear
  (was grey tabby). Coat is solid white (tabby stripes removed for this
  look).

Settings:
- **Yard** (new): grassy lawn, picket fence, a shade tree back-left, a
  low hedge front-right to hop over, sun in the sky.
- **Living room** (reused from the Funny Fights video, for continuity —
  same house, same "universe").

## Tone notes
- Zero conflict, zero danger — this is the deliberate opposite of the
  Funny Fights video: soft chime stings instead of record-scratches,
  gentle bounce instead of dust-cloud brawls, nuzzling instead of
  dazed X-eyes.
- The hedge-hop in the chase scene reuses the jump/squash-stretch
  system built for the fight video's dash and brawl scenes, just aimed
  at a purely joyful beat instead of a collision.
- Ends on a warm, sittable pose (both facing camera, content) rather
  than a cliffhanger — this is a "cute compilation" style ending.

## Audio plan
All SFX and music are synthesized in `src/audio_bff.py` (no
licensed/sourced audio) — warm chimes, an upbeat bouncy chase jingle,
soft nom/crunch sounds, and a cozy outro, built from the same waveform
synthesis approach as the Funny Fights soundtrack but tuned brighter
and gentler (major-key melodies, softer envelopes).
