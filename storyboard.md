# "Dog & Cat: Funny Fights" — Storyboard

Original short-form animated comedy, ~40 seconds, 24 fps, 1280x720 (16:9).
Style: flat vector cartoon, bold outlines, bright colors — think simple
"greeting card" cartoon dog (golden, floppy ears) vs. sleek grey cat.
No real footage used anywhere — everything is code-drawn.

Characters:
- **Rex** — floppy-eared golden dog, big goofy grin, stubby tail that
  wags fast when excited.
- **Whiskers** — smug grey tabby cat, half-closed eyes, twitchy tail.

Setting: a simple living room — rug, couch, small side table with a
potted plant, a squeaky bone toy on the floor.

## Tone notes
- Nothing mean-spirited or violent — this is slapstick, not aggression.
  No bared teeth, no blood, no real-world injury framing. Think Tom &
  Jerry / Looney Tunes energy: bouncy, exaggerated, harmless.
- Motion should read as *play-fighting*, reinforced by the ending beat
  (they end up side-by-side, tails wagging/twitching contentedly).
- Loopable: the final frame's pose rhymes with the opening standoff pose,
  so the video can loop cleanly if a viewer replays it.

## Audio plan
All SFX and music are synthesized in `src/audio.py` (no licensed/sourced
audio) — comic boings, squeaks, a bouncy kazoo-style chase riff, and a
xylophone-style outro jingle, built from simple waveform synthesis.
