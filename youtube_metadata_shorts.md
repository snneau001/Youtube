## Why a Short, and what it can realistically do
Shorts get pushed into a discovery feed shown to people who have never
seen your channel — that's the only realistic path to traction on a
brand-new channel with 0 subscribers. A single video is not going to
"guarantee" a hit; what actually moves the needle over time:
- **The first 1-2 seconds** — this cut starts mid-action with the title
  slamming in, not a slow build, because Shorts viewers swipe away fast.
- **Loop-ability** — 26.5s means an easy full watch-through, and a
  replay if it loops, both of which help average view duration.
- **Consistency** — one video rarely breaks out; channels that grow
  post repeatedly (the algorithm needs signal across multiple uploads).
- **Thumbnail/title clarity** — "Dog & Cat Best Friends" is instantly
  understood at a glance, which matters even though Shorts mostly
  autoplay without a thumbnail click.
